## 4. 自创/高度关联的术语和概念

### 4.1 Product Discovery（产品发现）
- **定义**: 在决定投入工程资源之前，通过快速原型和实验来发现可用、有价值、可行的解决方案的过程。
- **首次提出**: 2007 年 SVPG 文章 *Product Discovery*
- **原文**: "I prefer to think of this phase as 'product discovery' more than 'requirements and design.' ... First, you need to discover whether there are real users out there that want this product. ... Second, you need to discover a product solution to this problem that is usable, useful, and feasible."
- **演进**: 在《INSPIRED》第二版和 2017 年的 *Beyond Lean and Agile* 中进一步发展为"持续发现"（continuous discovery）—— discovery 和 delivery 并行双轨。
- **与后续者的关系**: Teresa Torres 的 *Continuous Discovery Habits* 是对此概念的扩展和具体方法论化。马蒂认可并推荐了该书。

### 4.2 Empowered Product Teams（授权产品团队）
- **定义**: 被赋予要解决的*问题*和期望的*业务成果*（而非功能和截止日期），并有权自主发现最佳解决方案的跨职能团队。
- **首次提出**: 2018 年 SVPG 文章 *Empowered Product Teams*
- **原文**: "The litmus test for empowerment is that the team is able to decide the best way to solve the problems they have been assigned (the objectives)."
- **与 Feature Teams 的对比**（2019 年文章细化）:
  - **Empowered Product Team**: 跨职能（PM + 设计师 + 工程师），以成果衡量，有权自主寻找解决方案
  - **Feature Team**: 被给予带优先级的 roadmap/feature 列表，本质上"服务业务方"，输出导向
  - **Delivery Team**: 最弱形态，即"dev team"/"scrum team"/"SAFe team"，PM 仅为 backlog 管理员

### 4.3 The Four Big Risks（四大风险）
- **定义**: 每个产品工作中必须应对的四类风险。
- **首次完整提出**: 2017 年 SVPG 文章 *The Four Big Risks*（在《INSPIRED》第二版中同时出现）
- **原文**:
  1. **Value risk** — "whether customers will buy it or users will choose to use it"
  2. **Usability risk** — "whether users can figure out how to use it"
  3. **Feasibility risk** — "whether our engineers can build what we need with the time, skills and technology we have"
  4. **Business viability risk** — "whether this solution also works for the various aspects of our business"
- **责任分工**:
  - PM 负责 value + viability
  - 设计师负责 usability
  - Tech Lead 负责 feasibility
- **关键的自我修正**: 在第一版《INSPIRED》(2008)中，他只列了三个属性（valuable, usable, feasible），其中 "valuable" 同时涵盖用户价值和商业价值。第二版 (2017) 明确将 viability 拆分为独立维度，因为他发现"产品经理只关注用户价值而忽视商业价值"是一个严重问题。

### 4.4 Product Operating Model / Product Model（产品运营模型）
- **定义**: 一套基于"第一性原理"的概念模型，指导领先产品公司如何创建产品。
- **首次系统化**: 2023 年 SVPG 文章 *The Product Operating Model: An Introduction*
- **三大维度**:
  1. How Products are Built — 小批量、高频、可靠的发布
  2. How Problems are Solved — 团队被赋予问题，自主寻找解决方案
  3. Deciding Which Problems to Solve — 领导层通过产品愿景和策略确定要解决哪些问题
- **五大产品概念**: Product Culture, Product Strategy, Product Teams, Product Discovery, Product Delivery
- **四大文化原则**: Principles over Process, Trust over Control, Innovation over Predictability, Learning over Failure

### 4.5 Missionaries vs. Mercenaries（传教士 vs. 雇佣兵）
- **来源**: 原概念引自 John Doerr（传奇风投），马蒂将其发展为产品团队文化的核心隐喻
- **首次使用**: 2015 年 SVPG 文章 *Missionaries vs. Mercenaries*
- **原文**: "Teams of missionaries are engaged, motivated, have a deep understanding of the business context, and tangible empathy for the customer. Teams of mercenaries feel no real sense of empowerment or accountability, no passion for the problem to be solved, and little real connection with the actual users and customers."
- **领导力归因**:
  1. Leadership — 领导者以为知道答案，只要团队服从指令
  2. Staffing — 团队由默认接受 mercenary 模式的人组成
  3. Process — SAFe 等企业级流程强化了 mercenary 模式

### 4.6 Product Manager vs. Product Owner
- **核心立场**: Product Owner 是 Scrum 中的行政管理角色（backlog administrator），只是 Product Manager 职责的极小一部分。
- **原文**（*Product Management - Start Here*）: "The product owner responsibilities are just a very small subset of the product manager responsibilities."
- **多次重申**（至少在以下文章中出现）:
  - *Behind Every Great Product* (2016)
  - *Product vs Feature Teams* (2019)
  - *Product Management - Start Here* (2020)

### 4.7 Product Creator（产品创造者）
- **首次提出**: 2025 年文章 *The Era of the Product Creator*
- **定义**: 任何积极塑造产品并应对产品风险（value, usability, feasibility, viability）的人——无论头衔。
- **核心观点**: AI 工具正在赋能更多设计师、工程师、创始人、产品领导者进入产品创造角色，传统的非创造型 PM 将面临淘汰。
- **原文**: "Rather, anyone actively shaping the product and tackling the product risks (value, usability, feasibility and viability) is a product creator. Their title doesn't really matter."

### 4.8 Build to Learn vs Build to Earn
- **首次提出**: 2026 年文章
- **核心区分**: 产品开发有两种模式——一种是"为了学习"（验证假设），一种是"为了赚钱"（交付价值）。在 AI 时代这个区分尤为重要。

### 4.9 Product Sense（产品感）
- 在 *The Era of the Product Creator* 中以链接形式提到（https://www.svpg.com/product-sense-demystified/），强调 product sense 是可培养的，不是天生的。

### 4.10 其他高频术语
- **Continuous Discovery and Delivery（持续发现与持续交付）**: 双轨模型，发现和交付并行
- **Durable Teams（持久团队）**: 反对项目制组队，主张团队长期存续
- **Outcomes over Output（成果优于产出）**: 等同于 "解决业务问题" 优于 "交付更多功能"
- **Stakeholders vs Product Teams**: 利益相关者提供约束条件，而非决定解决方案
- **Product Vision / Product Strategy / Team Topology**: 领导者必须提供的战略上下文三件套

## 5. 反复出现 ≥3 次的核心论点（真信念）

以下论点在马蒂不同年份、不同媒介（书、文章、演讲）反复出现，可视为其真正的智识信念:

### 信念 1: 最好的产品公司之所以不同，不是因为人才更优秀，而是因为工作方式不同
- 出现位置: *INSPIRED* (2008/2017), *EMPOWERED* (2020), *Empowered Product Teams* (2018)
- 典型原文: "But the real advantage these companies have is not so much who they hire, but rather how they enable their people to work together to solve hard problems."

### 信念 2: PM 的核心职责是确保 value 和 viability，不是 project management
- 出现位置: *Behind Every Great Product* (2016), *Product vs Feature Teams* (2019), *Product Management - Start Here* (2020), *The Era of the Product Creator* (2025), *The Four Big Risks* (2017)
- 典型原文: "The product manager is explicitly responsible for ensuring value and viability."
- 变体: PM 是 "CEO of the product"（但有争议，见矛盾记录）

### 信念 3: 团队应该被分配问题去解决，而不是功能去建造
- 出现位置: *Beyond Lean and Agile* (2017), *Empowered Product Teams* (2018), *Product vs Feature Teams* (2019), *EMPOWERED* (2020), *TRANSFORMED* (2023)
- 典型原文: "Rather than being given features to build, you've been given problems to solve."

### 信念 4: 产品发现必须在投入大规模工程之前完成
- 出现位置: *Product Discovery* (2007), *Beyond Lean and Agile* (2017), *The Four Big Risks* (2017), *INSPIRED 2nd Ed.* (2017)
- 典型原文: "Risks are tackled up front, rather than at the end."
- 持续强化: 用原型（prototype）而非产品（product）来测试和学习

### 信念 5: SAFe 等"企业级敏捷"框架与真正的产品创新不兼容
- 出现位置: *Missionaries vs. Mercenaries* (2015) — "I literally don't know of a single strong product company that uses [SAFe]"; *Product vs Feature Teams* (2019); *Product Management - Start Here* (2020) — "If your company is using a process like SAFe, and you want to be a true product manager... you'll likely have to move to a company that knows the difference"
- 原文: "I have a hard time imagining a worse model for true, technology-powered product companies that depend on a stream of consistent innovation."

### 信念 6: 产品经理、设计师和工程师必须紧密协作（collaborative），而非串行工作
- 出现位置: *Beyond Lean and Agile* (2017), *INSPIRED* (2017), *Behind Every Great Product* (2016) — "great products require an intense collaboration with design and engineering"
- 典型原文: "In strong teams, product, design and engineering work side-by-side, in a give and take."

### 信念 7: Coaching（教练）是管理者最重要的职责
- 出现位置: *EMPOWERED* (2020), *Empowered Product Teams* (2018), *Product Management - Start Here* (2020), Product Coaching 页面
- 典型原文: "Probably the single most important, yet most often overlooked, element to capable management is coaching."

### 信念 8: 招人看能力（competence）+ 品格（character），而不是"文化匹配"（cultural fit）
- 出现位置: *Empowered Product Teams* (2018)，引用 Stephen Covey（信任 = 能力 + 品格）+ Bob Sutton（"No Asshole Rule"）
- 典型原文: "The 'No Assholes Rule.' They understand that it doesn't matter how exceptionally skilled a player or coach may be, if he's an asshole, then he will be toxic."

### 信念 9: 产品经理必须是"公司最强的才能之一"，CEO 应将 PM 视为未来领导者
- 出现位置: *Behind Every Great Product* (2016), *Product vs Feature Teams* (2019), *INSPIRED*
- 典型原文: "the product manager needs to be among the strongest talent in the company"

### 信念 10: 内部产品 vs 商业产品有本质区别，做商业产品更难
- 出现位置: *Commercial vs Internal Products* (2026), *INSPIRED*
- 典型原文: "Internal Products Are Hard; Commercial Products Are Harder"

## 8. 发现的矛盾与待调和之处

### 矛盾 1: "PM 是产品的 CEO" 这个说法
马蒂在多处使用此比喻（*Behind Every Great Product*, 2016）：
> "The role I would argue that the product manager is most similar to is the role of the CEO. But with the obvious difference that unlike the CEO, nobody reports to the product manager."

但同时他也在多篇文章中强调 PM 没有直接汇报权、必须通过影响力来领导。这个比喻在 2024-2026 年的文章中逐渐淡化，被 "product creator" 替代。

**记录**: "CEO of the product" 这个说法在行业内引起过争议，部分人认为它放大了 PM 的自我重要性并模糊了协作本质。马蒂自己的写作中可以找到支持此比喻和削弱此比喻的证据。

### 矛盾 2: PM 的边界 — "do your homework" vs "don't do the designer's job"
马蒂在 *Product Management - Start Here* (2020) 中明确说设计工具和用户研究是设计师的职责:
> "While learning to use the tools of design is easy, learning to be a good designer is anything but easy. Second, if you spend your time doing the designer role, then who is going to do the product management role?"

但同时在 *The Era of the Product Creator* (2025) 中，随着 AI 工具发展，他鼓励更多设计师和工程师承担产品创造角色。这可能导致角色边界进一步模糊——如果设计师可以成为 product creator，PM 为什么不能做设计？

**记录**: 此为潜在的演变而非直接矛盾。2025 年的文章代表了一种开放态度，即在 AI 赋能下 "product creator" 的角色可以来自任何职能。

### 矛盾 3: "Innovation over Predictability" vs "Business Viability"
产品模型四大文化原则之一是 "Innovation over Predictability"。但四大风险中的 business viability risk 本质上是对可预测性和商业可持续性的追求。如何处理创新冲动与商业纪律之间的张力，马蒂没有给出很具体的平衡机制。

**记录**: 这是一个隐含张力，在现有文章中未见正面解决。

### 矛盾 4: 对 "MVP" 的态度
在 *Beyond Lean and Agile* (2017) 中批评很多团队对 MVP 的误用:
> "I meet countless teams that claim to be following Lean principles, yet they work for months on what they call an 'MVP' and they really don't know what they have."

而在 *The Purpose of Prototypes* (2025) 和 *Build to Learn vs Build to Earn* (2026) 中，他推广了 "build to learn" 的概念，与 Lean Startup 的 MVP 原意非常接近。

**记录**: 他不是反对 MVP，而是反对对 MVP 的曲解（把一个完整产品称为 MVP 然后花数月时间）。他的 "prototype" 概念实际上就是对 MVP 原始含义（最小可行产品来学习）的回归。
