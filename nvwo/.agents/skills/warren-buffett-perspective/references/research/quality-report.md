## 4.1 Sanity Check

### 问题1: 比特币是好的投资吗？

**Skill回答**：
（以巴菲特身份模拟）
"I wouldn't touch it. Bitcoin doesn't produce anything. You're buying a piece of digital paper and hoping someone else pays more for it tomorrow. That's not investing — that's speculation. I don't know how to value something that doesn't generate earnings. I've said many times: if you buy an asset that will never produce cash flow, the only way to make money is if someone else pays more than you did. That's the greater fool theory. I don't own any Bitcoin. I never will. It's not an asset, it's a赌注."

**真实立场**：Rat poison squared（老鼠药平方），强烈反对。巴菲特在2018年伯克希尔股东会上称比特币是"rat poison squared"，在多个场合表示比特币不产生任何价值，是纯粹的投机。

**方向一致？**：是。SKILL通过"能力圈"模型（不懂不投）+ "不投不懂的东西"启发式 + "赌博式投机"反模式，自然推导出与真实立场一致的结论。

### 问题2: 普通投资者应该买什么？

**Skill回答**：
（以巴菲特身份模拟）
"You know, I've said this many times. For the average person who doesn't have the time or desire to study businesses — and that's most people, and there's nothing wrong with that — the best thing they can do is buy a low-cost S&P 500 index fund. Every month. Through thick and thin. Don't try to time the market. Don't pick individual stocks unless you really know what you're doing. A simple index fund will beat most professional money managers over time. Charlie and I have a bet that proved this. I've even said in my will that 90% of my wife's inheritance should go into an S&P 500 index fund."

**真实立场**：低成本的标普500指数基金。巴菲特从1993年起多次推荐普通投资者买指数基金，2007年与Protege Partners的百万美元赌局（标普500指数基金10年跑赢5只对冲基金组合）已验证。2014年股东信中明确遗嘱安排：90%资金买Vanguard S&P 500指数基金。

**方向一致？**：是。SKILL虽未显式列出指数基金推荐（可作为额外补充），但通过复利与长期持有模型 + "买企业不买股票"的框架，可以正确推导。建议在决策启发式中补充此条，使其更直接可查。

### 问题3: 股票回购是好事吗？

**Skill回答**：
（以巴菲特身份模拟）
"It depends on the price. If a company's stock is selling well below its intrinsic value, buying back shares is the best thing management can do for remaining shareholders. We do it at Berkshire when the price is right. In fact, when we buy back our own stock below intrinsic value, every remaining shareholder gets a little richer. But if a company buys overpriced stock — just to boost EPS or meet Wall Street expectations — they're destroying value. I've seen CEOs who buy stock at any price because they want to look like they're doing something. That's not smart, that's foolish."

**真实立场**：如果价格低于内在价值，是好事。巴菲特在多个股东信中（特别是2011年及之后的回购政策解读）详细阐述：回购只有在价格明显低于保守估计的内在价值时才对股东有利。

**方向一致？**：是。SKILL通过"价格是你支付的，价值是你得到的"启发式 + 安全边际模型 + 管理层质量模型，能正确区分有价值的回购和有害的回购。

## 4.2 Edge Case

### 问题：你怎么看量子计算对投资的影响？

**Skill回答**：
（以巴菲特身份模拟）
"You know, I'm going to be honest with you. I don't understand quantum computing well enough to have a strong opinion about it. I'm 95 years old, and this is one of those things that might as well be magic to me. But let me tell you what I do know. When a technology is that new and that complex, almost nobody — including the people selling it — can tell you which companies will win and which will lose. History is full of transformative technologies that made a few people very rich and destroyed a lot of capital along the way. I've seen the same pattern with the internet, with biotech, with every new thing. The businesses that will benefit from quantum computing may not even exist yet. And the ones that exist today, priced as if they've already won — that's a dangerous game. I'd rather wait and see who builds a real moat around it before I put money in."

**评估**：
- 诚实边界运用正确：明确承认"I don't understand"。
- 能力圈框架有效：回到已知的历史类比（互联网、生物科技等新技术周期）。
- 没有过度确定性：没有假装懂量子计算，而是在已知框架内推理。
- 内在张力体现："我自己也没想清楚的"中明确提到"不能预测我对全新领域的反应"，符合此场景。

**Pass**：Skill的诚实边界机制（第6条诚实边界 + 表达DNA的"I don't know... but here's what I do know"模式）在此场景得到正确应用。

## 总结
